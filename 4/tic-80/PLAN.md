## Phase 0b: Design pass

Originally scoped as six paper decisions (playfield scale, palette, sprite roster, music/SFX, new mechanics, screen layout) written up before any code existed. Revised after the playfield/screen-layout conversation itself surfaced two non-obvious findings pure derivation almost missed — a wide enemy tier needs a band restricted to ~75°-88° to avoid sitting below the ship, and the existing size-by-radius formula would render that same tier backwards (biggest sprite, farthest away) if ported as-is. Paper math got this far; the rest is more reliably found by building and looking than by further derivation. **Design pass now proceeds as a proof-of-concept build**, in the order below, with DESIGN.md written incrementally from what the POC actually validates rather than from what was merely proposed. The POC's own code is the seed for the real cart, not throwaway — Phase 1 onward (Step 7) gets rewritten from what exists once Stages A-C land, not built from scratch.

Numbers proposed so far (shared centre (120,56), ship radius 70, tiers at 24/36/48/100, ~40 wave-1 enemies) were Stage A's starting point going in — the first item that actually moved once rendered was the tiers' angular bands, see Stage A below and DESIGN.md's Core system design → Enemy tiers section.

### Stage B: Movement & waves

- [x] Port ship arc movement + hold-to-fire, adapted to the new centre/radius/trim. Went through two redesigns during live play-testing, not a straight port: first to a continuous up/down radius (in toward the horizon, out to an edge-hugging bound), then to a fixed stack of elliptical lanes (`LANES`) after a play-test note explained a circle can't touch both the playfield's bottom-centre and its left/right edges at once — only an ellipse can, and only if the trim angle is exactly ±90° (not pico-8's ±69°). Lane switching eases quickly rather than teleporting or crawling ("slide, but more like a snap"). Two follow-on fixes came out of the same redesign: player shots used to aim along a radial direction that only pointed at the centre dot on a circle, not an ellipse (now aims at the literal point instead), and ship/alien size used to be tied to lane/tier rather than actual on-screen position (now `size_by_y()`/`alien_size_by_y()`, continuous, decoupled from which lane or tier something occupies — aliens specifically use a two-segment version, tiny-to-medium from the entry points to the horizon, only slightly larger past it, per a play-test note)
- [x] Port enemy sweep behaviour per tier — tier 4's restricted-band sweep is new logic, not a direct port of the other three tiers'. Also ported pico-8's two-phase entrance (orbit an entry point, then migrate into formation), initially skipped and then reinstated after a live smoke-test showed why it's load-bearing: without it, enemies fire from their formation position immediately at wave start and can empty all 5 lives before any input matters (many sit near ea=0, firing straight down the same column the ship's rest position occupies). Live play-testing asked for the entrance much slower, with more circling and lateral drift, and varying wave to wave — implemented as several entrance parameters (orbit radius, lateral sway amplitude, orbit direction, circle-vs-migrate split) randomized fresh per wave in `spawn_wave` rather than fixed constants. A real bug surfaced once a wave actually cleared for the first time: `spawn_wave()`'s respawned enemies had no `x`/`y` until `update_game()`'s next pass, but a wave-clear calls `spawn_wave()` from inside `update_game()` itself, after that frame's enemy loop already ran — so the fresh enemies hit that same frame's draw call with a nil position and crashed. Fixed by seeding `x,y` at the entry point when an enemy is created
- [x] Wire up wave spawning at the ~40-enemy wave-1 target (39 at current TIERS counts, scaling per wave); no performance issues observed in live testing at this scale
- [~] Decide/tune the difficulty ramp (enemy count growth, sweep speed, fire/dive frequency per wave) against what actually plays — underway via live play-testing, not finished. Confirmed so far: enemy-shot damage split from lives into a fractional cost (0.1/shot, 1/dive-hit) rather than pico-8's flat -1-either-way, after a smoke-test showed the flat cost draining all 5 lives almost immediately; formation sweep speed (`ESPD_BASE`) halved after a play-test note called it too fast. The rest (`WFC_BASE`, `DIVE_SPD`, dive chance, wave-count scaling) are still placeholder-shaped guesses per 4.lua's own comments, not yet confirmed by extended play
